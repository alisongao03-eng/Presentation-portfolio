Approach

Narrative first

The argument comes before the layout. Every deck begins with a single clear storyline; the pages are simply vessels for the narrative.

Evidence & rigour

Benchmarks, policy references and data sources are traceable on every page — so decision-makers can trust the substance as much as the styling.

Crafted restraint

One typographic hierarchy, one measured shade of gold. Refinement comes from restraint, never from ornament piled on ornament.
